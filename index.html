How SyncUp Works

SyncUp Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

SyncUp is designed as a privacy-respecting peer-to-peer collaboration platform for developers, student builders, and open source creators.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential profile details you provide: username, email address, bio, skills, and portfolio links. Private subcollections protect your email and direct message applications.

2. How Data Is Used

Your public information powers team matching and project discovery. We do not sell or monetize personal developer data.

3. Data Security & Storage

Project images are compressed before upload to prevent quota abuse and keep storage footprints minimal. User authentication is secured via Firebase Auth.
